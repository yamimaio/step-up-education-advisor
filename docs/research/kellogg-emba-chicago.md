## PART 2: Chicago schedule (January 2027 first-year calendar)

Source: https://www.kellogg.northwestern.edu/programs/executive-mba/locations-and-schedule/chicago-twice-monthly/, checked 2026-10-10. Asterisked weekends "may include Sunday classes".

| Session | Dates | Days counted |
|---|---|---|
| Orientation | Programming January 19-24, 2027 (check-in January 18) | 6 |
| Class sessions | February 5-7, February 19-21, March 5-7, March 19-21, April 9-11, April 23-25, May 21-23, June 4-6, October 1-3, October 15-17 | 10 x 3 = 30 |
| Class sessions | May 7-8, September 17-18, October 29-30, November 12-13 | 4 x 2 = 8 |
| Intensive week | June 21-25, 2027 | 5 |
| Global Network Week | August 2027, Evanston: "Students based on U.S. campuses (Miami or Chicago) take courses for 5 days." (https://www.kellogg.northwestern.edu/programs/executive-mba/curriculum-and-faculty/global-network-week/) | 5 |

- Days: 6 + 38 + 5 + 5 = **54**, counting each listed date, Sundays included where the calendar lists them.
- Trips: orientation, 14 class sessions, the intensive week and Global Network Week = **17**.
- Longest stretch: **6 days**, orientation.
- The second-year calendar is not published.
- Campus: Wieboldt Hall, "340 E. Superior St. Chicago, Illinois 60611" (https://www.kellogg.northwestern.edu/facilities/wieboldt-hall/).

## Uncertain or conflicting

- Sundays: asterisked weekends "may include Sunday classes"; the count includes every date the calendar lists, so it may be a day or two high if a listed Sunday has no class.
- The second-year calendar is not published, so year 1 stands for both years.
- The class profile, cost and curriculum are shared with Miami and combine both campuses.
