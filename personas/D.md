# Persona D: The EMBA case ("a VP move that needs a degree")

## Who they are

Fourteen years in, nine leading, a director in San Francisco. The VP role they want is at a company that expects a recognised graduate degree, and they want classmates who are already senior, in a hub where those people live. Employer sponsorship is likely.

## In their voice

- Goal: "The VP role I want is at a company that asks for a graduate degree. It isn't a formality there."
- What is missing: "The degree, and honestly the people. I want to know the directors and VPs in this city."
- On time: "Two years is fine if I keep my job."

## Expected verdict

- `goalClarity`: clear
- Category: executive MBA. Hand-computed: a required degree rules out executive programs, certificates and short courses; the EMBA scores 12 (degree 6, network 4, leadership 2) against 8 for a full-time MBA.
- The record in the data is Wharton's San Francisco program, which is every-other-weekend in person rather than hybrid. Many of its figures (duration, on-site days, hours) are not published, so expect near misses and low confidence.
- Tension to raise: none.

## What a sharp advisor should notice

- The reflection beat is the one in the plan: they need a network in a hub and a degree their next employer recognises. Say it nearly that way.
- The degree is required, so executive programs are out regardless of how good they are. Say that, so the user can check the logic.
- The tuition on the Wharton record is the 2026 class price and its class profile is for a different year; the card should carry those caveats.
