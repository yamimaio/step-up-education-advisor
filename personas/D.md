## What a sharp advisor should notice

- The reflection beat is the one in the plan: they need a network in a hub and a degree their next employer recognises. Say it nearly that way.
- The degree is required, so executive programs are out regardless of how good they are. Say that, so the user can check the logic.
- The tuition on the Wharton record is the 2026 class price and its class profile is for a different year; the card should carry those caveats.
