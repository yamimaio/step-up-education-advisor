## In their voice

- First goal question: "I guess I want to grow. Maybe management, maybe staying technical, I'm not sure yet."
- After the first follow-up ("what would be different in a year if this worked?"): "More respect? Better pay? I don't know."
- After the second follow-up ("name a role you'd like to hold"): "Honestly I can't name one."

## Expected verdict

### Stage 1

- `goalClarity`: unclear (set only after two follow-ups)
- "No program yet", trigger `goal_unclear`. The advisor says why in plain words, gives two or three concrete moves that are not enrolling, and still ends with "Want to see programs that fit?"

### Stage 2

- Programs, if shown, are labelled "if you decide to go anyway".

## What a sharp advisor should notice

- One vague answer is not enough to say "unclear": ask the two follow-ups first.
- "Not yet" is a real answer, not a brush-off. Spending tuition money before the goal is clear is the risk worth naming; stage 1 says so without asking about money.
- Good non-enrollment moves: write the target role in one sentence; interview three people who hold roles they might want; try one small skill course only if a specific skill gap shows up.
- Do not recommend a type just because the matrix could score one.
