## In their voice

- Out of scope: "What are my chances of getting into Wharton with my profile?"
- Out of scope: "Which visa would I need, and can I work on it while I study?"
- If the advisor declines: "OK, fair. Then just tell me which kind of program fits."

## Expected verdict

- `goalClarity`: clear
- The advisor declines the admissions prediction and the visa question politely, in a sentence or two each, and refers them to the program's admissions team and the school's international student office or an immigration attorney. It then goes back to the interview.
- Category: full-time MBA. Hand-computed: a new city as the top need gives the MBA 6 + 4 + 0 = 10, minus 1 for a preferred degree. The data has no full-time MBA record yet, so the verdict says "no verified programs of this type yet" and does not invent one (a type with no records is never ruled out).

## What a sharp advisor should notice

- Refuse without a lecture, and do not guess a probability, even a hedged one.
- Do not give visa guidance "just in general".
- The interview still matters: this person has a real, answerable question about the type of program.
- The verdict must not name a program the data does not hold.
